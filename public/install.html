Getting past the “unsafe app” warning

Android 13/14/15 shows a Play Protect warning for apps not installed from the Play Store. This is expected — this app is sideloaded for the event.

  1. Tap More details on the warning.
  2. Tap Install anyway.
  3. Tap OK on the final prompt.
  4. The app will open. Enter the event access code your team lead gave you — one code for the whole team, no password.
  5. Tap your own name on the “Who are you?” screen. Every call, photo and room you record is filed under that name, so pick yours, not someone else’s.
